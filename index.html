Classic games in 3D

Playable in the browser, no install. Add it to your home screen to use it as an app and play offline.

♞

Chess

Rules from chess.js (castling, en passant, check, mate, draws). Pawns promote to a queen. Weak computer opponent or two players.

Checkers

American rules with forced captures, multi-jumps and kings.

Connect Four

Ice against fire: drop discs, get four in a row. Computer or two players.

One Minute Pop

Tap the targets for 60 seconds (2D).

Slow phone? Use the Effects button inside a game to switch to lite (no ambient particles, fewer sparks, lower resolution). It is remembered.
